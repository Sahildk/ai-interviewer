# Nexus AI Interviewer - Hackathon Demo Script

**Target Duration:** 2 minutes
**Tone:** Professional, energetic, and problem-focused.

---

## 0:00 - 0:20 | The Problem (Face Cam or Title Slide)
"Hi, I'm [Your Name].

We've all been there—you have the technical skills, but you freeze up during the actual interview. The problem isn't knowledge; it's **articulation** and **confidence**.

Current mock interview platforms are either text-based, expensive, or just don't feel real. That's why I built **Nexus AI Interviewer**."

---

## 0:20 - 0:35 | The Setup (Screen Recording)
*(Show the Landing/Setup Page)*

"Nexus is a hyper-realistic, voice-enabled interview simulator powered by Google's Gemini models.

Here on the dashboard, I can customize my session. I'll choose a **Technical** interview at a **Mid-Senior** level.
Crucially, I can paste my actual **Resume** or a job description here. This gives the AI full context to ask relevant, tailored questions—just like a real recruiter."

*(Action: Paste a resume summary, click "Initialize Simulation")*

---

## 0:35 - 1:10 | The Interview (Main Feature)
*(Show the Chat Interface)*

"Meet Alex, my AI interviewer. Unlike standard chatbots, Alex uses a voice-first approach."

*(Action: Let the AI ask the first question. e.g., "Tell me about a challenging bug you fixed.")*

"I can speak naturally using my microphone."

*(Action: Click Mic, speak a short answer. e.g., "I once had a race condition in a React app...", click Send)*

"Notice the latency is near-instant. The AI analyzes my response not just for correctness, but for clarity. If I give a vague answer, it pushes back with a follow-up question, simulating the pressure of a real deep-dive."

---

## 1:10 - 1:35 | The Analytics (The "Wow" Factor)
*(Action: Click "End Interview", transition to Report Page)*

"Once the session is over, I get an immediate, comprehensive **Performance Report**.

It breaks down my performance into three key metrics:
1.  **Technical Accuracy**
2.  **Communication Style**
3.  **Culture Fit**

It highlights my key strengths and actually gives me specific, actionable advice on what to improve—no generic feedback here."

---

## 1:35 - 2:00 | Conclusion & Stack
*(Scroll down the report or show a closing slide)*

"Nexus AI was built using **Next.js 15**, **Tailwind**, and a **Node.js** backend orchestrating **Gemini 1.5 Flash** for low-latency reasoning.

It bridges the gap between practice and the real thing, helping engineers find their voice before it counts.

Thank you!"
